About

I’m a recent Computer Science graduate from the University of Calgary with an interest in software engineering, data analysis, and machine learning. I enjoy building practical projects that combine technical problem-solving with real-world use cases, from full-stack applications and database-driven systems to data analysis and custom machine learning models.

Background

My professional experience has given me a mix of technical support and data-focused problem-solving. In IT support, I worked directly with users to troubleshoot hardware, software, and connectivity issues, maintain computer systems, and explain technical concepts in a clear and practical way.

I later worked in operations and data analysis, where I used SQL, spreadsheets, dashboards, and workflow systems to analyze production data, track operational performance, and improve visibility across business processes. These experiences have influenced how I approach software projects today: understanding the problem first, designing a clear workflow, and then building a solution around it.
